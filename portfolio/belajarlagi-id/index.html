Projects's Challenge

How might we restructure and redesign Belajarlagi.id website so that bootcamp information is presented more concisely and follows a clearer flow for prospective learners?

Project's Process

The existing website needed a clearer way to communicate the bootcamp offering. The revamp focused on improving the flow and presentation of information so visitors could understand the programs and supporting content without being overwhelmed by the amount of information.

The project involved requirements analysis followed by UI/UX design and coordination with the client. The design work focused on translating the required information into a clearer website structure and interface.

...

Results

The revamp produced a redesigned Belajarlagi.id website experience with a clearer presentation of the bootcamp offering. The program pages were structured to present the available information in a more organized way, making it easier for users to scan, understand, and navigate the content.

The final design covered the main website experience from the introduction of Belajarlagi.id and its value proposition through program information, registration-related content, testimonials, supporting information, FAQs, and contact details.
